Why Karak honey

The land Pakistan
thanks for its honey

Karak, in the south of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, is famous across Pakistan for its honey. Its dry, sun-warmed hills and hardy wild flora give the bees a harsher, purer forage — and the honey a depth of flavour the green plains can't match. When people in Pakistan want real honey, they ask for Karak's.
